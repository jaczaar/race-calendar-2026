# Sail Esprit Montreal Olympic Triathlon Fit

Working date: September 12, 2026.

Role in the year plan: fun, controlled Olympic-distance triathlon. No PR attempt and no dedicated taper.

Fit:
- Follows HYROX Washington DC by roughly one week.
- Works as a local race weekend and a bridge back into the fall running block.
- Swim and bike can be honest if recovery is good.
- Run should stay controlled if legs are still carrying HYROX fatigue.
- Should not compromise the two-half-marathon structure or Beirut Marathon build.

Planning note:
Keep this as a low-pressure race. If HYROX DC causes unusual soreness or travel fatigue, race Sail Esprit as aerobic tempo rather than forcing Olympic-tri performance.
